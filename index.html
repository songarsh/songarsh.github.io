Bio

I am a Master’s student at ETH Zürich in the Electrical Engineering and Information Technology program, working with the SAFARI Research Group under Prof. Onur Mutlu.

My research sits at the intersection of computer architecture and memory systems. I am particularly drawn to rethinking the memory hierarchy to make future computing both more capable and more sustainable.

Before ETH, I spent two and a half years at Samsung Semiconductor architecting dedicated compute for AI workloads and contributing to the HBM-PIM near-memory architecture. I earned my Bachelor’s in Electronics & Communication Engineering from R. V. College of Engineering, Bengaluru.

Feel free to reach out — I’m always happy to chat about memory systems, hardware research, or anything in between.
